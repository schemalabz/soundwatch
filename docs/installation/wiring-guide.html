Soundwatch Sensor Wiring Guide

Per-sensor power assembly. Follow steps in order. Each step completes before the next.

230V MAINS MUST BE DISCONNECTED during all wiring/soldering steps.
Only connect to mains at Step 7 (testing). If you need to change anything, disconnect mains first.

Step 0: Know Your Wires

Two separate circuits: 230V AC (dangerous) and 5V DC (safe). The converter bridges them.

230V AC Side (inside junction box)
L (fasi)
= Live — 230V, the dangerous one. Goes through the fuse, then to IRM-10-5 AC/L pin.
N (oudeteros)
= Neutral — return path. Goes straight to IRM-10-5 AC/N pin. No fuse.
5V DC Side (USB cable to sensor)
Red (+5V)
= +5V DC — from IRM-10-5 +V pin. Soldered to USB cable red wire.
Black (GND)
= Ground — from IRM-10-5 -V pin. Soldered to USB cable black wire.
USB cable has 4 wires inside: Red (+5V), Black (GND), White (D+), Green (D-). We only use red and black for power. The white and green data wires are not needed — cut them short and wrap with electrical tape.

Wiring Steps

We prep the USB cable first, then solder DC side, then AC side, then assemble.

1
Prepare the USB Cable
  1. Cut the USB-A end off the cable (keep the micro USB end intact)
  2. Strip back ~3cm of the outer cable jacket to expose the 4 inner wires
  3. Identify the wires: Red (+5V), Black (GND), White (D+), Green (D-)
  4. Cut the white and green data wires short (~5mm) and wrap with electrical tape
  5. Strip ~5mm of insulation from the red and black wires
  6. Slide heat shrink tubing onto both wires NOW (before soldering — you can't add it later!)
Don't forget the heat shrink! Slide a ~15mm piece of heat shrink onto each wire BEFORE soldering. Push it back from the end so it doesn't shrink during soldering.
2
Solder DC Output — USB Cable to IRM-10-5

Use the third hand tool to hold the IRM-10-5 module steady. Solder the USB wires to the DC output pins:

IRM-10-5 — DC Output Pins (right side)
+V Pin
Red wire
USB +5V
-V Pin
Black wire
USB GND
  1. Tin the IRM-10-5 +V pin with a small blob of solder
  2. Solder the red USB wire to the +V pin
  3. Tin the IRM-10-5 -V pin
  4. Solder the black USB wire to the -V pin
  5. Gently tug each wire to confirm solid joint
  6. Slide heat shrink over each joint, apply heat gun
IRM-10-5 pin layout (bottom view): Left side has AC/L (top) and AC/N (bottom). Right side has -V (top) and +V (bottom). Check the printed labels on your module — they may vary slightly.
3
Wire the Fuse Holder

The fuse goes inline on the Live (L) wire only. It protects the circuit if something shorts.

  1. Cut two pieces of red 0.5mm² wire, each ~10cm long
  2. Strip ~5mm from each end of both wires
  3. Slide heat shrink onto both wires before soldering
  4. Solder one wire to one end of the fuse holder
  5. Solder the other wire to the other end of the fuse holder
  6. Apply heat shrink to both solder joints
  7. Insert the 1.6A glass fuse into the holder
The fuse holder now has two free wire ends: One will connect to the klema L terminal, the other will solder to the IRM-10-5 AC/L pin. It doesn't matter which end goes where — a fuse works in either direction.
4
Solder AC Input — Fuse + Neutral to IRM-10-5

Connect the 230V AC wires to the IRM-10-5 input pins:

IRM-10-5 — AC Input Pins (left side)
AC/L Pin
From fuse holder
One end of fuse assembly
AC/N Pin
Black 0.5mm² wire
Goes to klema N
  1. Solder one wire from the fuse holder assembly to the AC/L pin
  2. Cut a ~10cm piece of black 0.5mm² wire for Neutral
  3. Solder it to the AC/N pin
  4. Apply heat shrink to both joints
Double-check pin labels! Connecting L and N to the wrong pins won't damage the converter (it's AC), but make sure you're on the AC side, not the DC side. AC pins are labeled AC/L and AC/N on the PCB.

You now have the complete power assembly:

Klema L → fuse holder wire → fuse → fuse holder wire → AC/L

Klema N → black wire → AC/N

+V → red USB wire → micro USB → SCK

-V → black USB wire → micro USB → SCK

5
Install Cable Gland on Junction Box
  1. Drill an M12 hole in the junction box wall (if no existing knock-out)
  2. Pass the USB cable (micro USB end first) through the cable gland
  3. Mount the cable gland in the hole
  4. Don't tighten fully yet — you may need to adjust cable length
The cable gland separates the two enclosures. The USB cable passes through it from the power box (junction box) to the sensor box. The gland provides strain relief and keeps the junction box sealed.
6
Pre-Power Checklist

Go through this checklist. Every item must be true before connecting to 230V mains.

  • Mains is disconnected — klema has no power
  • Fuse holder: one wire goes to AC/L, other wire is free for klema L
  • Neutral wire: soldered to AC/N, other end free for klema N
  • 1.6A fuse is inserted in the fuse holder
  • USB red wire soldered to +V pin
  • USB black wire soldered to -V pin
  • White and green USB wires are cut and taped
  • All 4 solder joints have heat shrink applied — no exposed copper
  • Gently tug every soldered wire — none come loose
  • Cable gland is mounted, USB cable passes through
  • No solder bridges or shorts visible on the IRM-10-5 pins
7
Power On & Test with Multimeter (No Sensor Yet)

Do NOT connect the SCK yet. First verify the output voltage.

  1. Connect the free AC wires to the klema: fuse wire → L, neutral wire → N
  2. Stand clear. Turn on mains power to the junction box
  3. Use NCV (non-contact voltage) mode on the multimeter to confirm the junction box is live
  4. Set multimeter to DC Volts
  5. Measure across the micro USB end — red probe on red wire (or VBUS pin), black probe on black wire (or GND pin)
  6. Reading should be ~5.0V (acceptable range: 4.9V - 5.1V)
If you read 0V: Turn off mains. Check: Is the fuse intact? Are all solder joints solid? Are you measuring DC, not AC? Are L and N connected to the klema?
If you smell burning, hear buzzing, or the IRM-10-5 gets very hot immediately: Disconnect mains instantly. Do not touch the module. Let it cool. Inspect for solder bridges or wrong pin connections.
8
Connect Sensor & Verify
  1. Disconnect mains
  2. Plug the micro USB cable into the Smart Citizen Kit 2.3
  3. Make sure the LiPo battery is connected to the SCK (JST connector)
  4. Re-connect mains power
  5. Watch for the SCK LED sequence (indicates boot)
  6. Measure voltage at the IRM-10-5 output again under load — should still read ~5V
  7. Leave running for 30-60 minutes
  8. Check IRM-10-5 temperature: warm is normal, too hot to touch is a problem
  9. Verify SCK doesn't reboot or lose power during the soak test
The SCK draws well under 2A. The IRM-10-5 is rated for 2A/10W continuous, so there's plenty of headroom. The LiPo battery acts as a UPS — if mains drops briefly, the SCK stays powered.

Success! If the SCK boots, stays online, and voltage is stable after the soak test, the power assembly is good. Tighten the cable gland, close the junction box, and proceed to sensor placement and WiFi configuration.
